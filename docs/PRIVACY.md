# VoiceBlueprint Pro — Privacy and Network Behavior

VoiceBlueprint Pro is a local-first open-source voice production application. Voice projects, audio, and model inference may run on the user's device when compatible local engines are configured. **Local-first does not mean that every feature is offline or that no network connections ever occur.**

The application can connect to the internet for explicit model/runtime downloads, updates, optional integrations, remote backends, external links and other configured services. The inherited codebase also includes analytics and diagnostics integrations; their actual activation, controls, and transmission must be reviewed before production distribution.

Do not upload another person's voice without authorization. Voice cloning and generated audio may be subject to local laws, platform rules, and model licenses.

This page describes the intended operating model and known audit obligations; it is not a claim that an independent network-security/privacy assessment has been completed. For source and issues see https://github.com/DevTunaSoft/VoiceBlueprint-Pro.
