# VoiceBlueprint Pro — Sponsorship status

**VoiceBlueprint Pro does not currently operate a verified donation, sponsorship, or paid-support program.**

The app is an independent DevTunaSoft project. The original project's sponsors, payment providers, and commercial customers are **not** automatically sponsors or customers of VoiceBlueprint Pro.

There are **no current VoiceBlueprint Pro sponsors** and no active sponsor tiers or checkout destinations. Do not send funds to third-party or original-project payment accounts expecting to support this project.

If a verified, independently owned program is launched, this page and the app's support screens will be updated together with accurate payment ownership, recognition terms, and privacy information.

For now, the best way to help is to review documentation, submit reproducible issues (when Issues are enabled), and contribute through the [project repository](https://github.com/DevTunaSoft/VoiceBlueprint-Pro).

The historical authors and contributors of incorporated open-source components are acknowledged in the project's licence and credits files. Those legal acknowledgments do not imply an ongoing business relationship.
